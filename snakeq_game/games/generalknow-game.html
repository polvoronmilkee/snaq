How to Play SnaQ - General Knowledge

🎯 Objective

Control the snake to eat colored apples with correct answers to English questions. Grow your snake and reach the target score!

🕹️ Controls

  • Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move the snake
  • Press SHIFT to sprint / move faster
  • Press SPACE to pause/unpause the game
  • Press R to restart the current game

🍎 Gameplay

  • Read the General Knowledge question at the top
  • Look at the A, B, C answer options below
  • Find the apple with the matching letter for the correct answer
  • Eat correct apples to grow and score points (+10)
  • Avoid wrong answers or lose points (-5) and a life
  • Don't bite yourself or you'll lose a life

🛡️ Power‑Ups

  • Shield: Spawns when you’re low on lives. Grab it to block one mistake!
  • Sprint Bar: Refills over time. Zoom carefully so you don’t miss apples.

❤️ Lives & Scoring

  • You start with 3 lives (hearts)
  • Lose a life for wrong answers or self-bites
  • Game ends when you lose all lives
  • Speed increases as you progress
