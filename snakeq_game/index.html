How to Play SnaQ

🎯 Choose Your Quest

Select Mathematics or English to test your knowledge while playing snake!

🕹️ Game Modes

  • Quiz Mode: Answer 10 questions correctly to win
  • Endless Mode: Keep playing and growing your snake
  • Timed Mode: Answer as many as you can in 1 minute

🍎 Gameplay

  • Control the snake with WASD or Arrow Keys
  • Read questions and eat the correct colored apple
  • Grow your snake and increase your score
  • Avoid wrong answers or lose lives
