How to Play SnaQ

🎯 Pick Your Adventure

Choose from Mathematics, English, Science, or General Knowledge. Answer questions to help your hungry snake grow!

🕹️ Controls

Use WASD or Arrow Keys to slither around. Hold SHIFT to turbo sprint (watch your energy!). Pause with ESC, resume with SPACE, or restart with R.

🍎 Your Mission

Read the question at the top and eat the colored apple that matches your answer! Correct answers make your snake grow longer, while wrong ones cost you a life.

🛡️ Power‑Ups & Features

Shield: Appears when you're low on lives - grab it to block one mistake!
Sprint Bar: Hold SHIFT to move faster (energy refills over time).
Volt Tech Skin: +10% max energy, -15% drain, +15% regen!
Shop System: Earn points to unlock new skins and accessories!
Fresh Questions: Always new trivia to challenge you!

🏆 Game Modes

Quiz: Get 10 right to win
Endless: How long can you grow?
Timed: 60 seconds of speedy snacking

💡 Think fast, slither smart, and have fun. Let's make your brain and snake super strong!
