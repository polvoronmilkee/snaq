How to Play SnaQ

🎯 Pick Your Adventure

Choose from Mathematics, English, Science, or General Knowledge. Answer questions to help your hungry snake grow!

🕹️ Controls

  • Use WASD or Arrow Keys to slither around
  • Hold SHIFT to turbo sprint (don't run out of energy!)
  • Press ESC to pause the game
  • Press SPACE to resume the game
  • Press R to restart the current game

🍎 Your Mission

  • Read the question at the top
  • Eat the colored apple that matches your answer choice!
  • Correct bites give points and make your snake longer
  • Wrong bites make your snake say "yuck!" and cost a life

🛡️ Power‑Ups & Special Features

  • Shield: Spawns when you're low on lives. Grab it to block one mistake!
  • Sprint Bar: Hold SHIFT to move faster. Energy refills over time.
  • Volt Tech Skin: Special skin with +10% max energy, -15% drain, +15% regen!
  • Shop System: Earn points to unlock new snake skins and accessories!
  • Fresh Questions: API integration provides varied trivia content!

🏆 Game Modes

  • Quiz: Get 10 right to win
  • Endless: How long can you grow?
  • Timed: 60 seconds of speedy snacking

Tip: Think fast, slither smart, and have fun. Let's make your brain and snake super strong!
